Role

Art Direction & Graphic System

Company

Vizz

Comenta Que Sales is back!. Our hosts experience the passion of live European football in RTVE's official digital broadcast of UEFA Euro 2024 in Germany.
The hosts humor and improvisation make the broadcast the most enjoyable and relaxed way to watch the match. During the pre-match, they will discuss their expectations and interact with the audience about their predictions.

In the post-match segment, each program features a different special guest who shares their impressions of the match and predicts the outcome of the next game.
Throughout the show, we'll connect with reporters on the ground in Germany who are experiencing the competition firsthand.

Creative Director — Jaime Sordo

Creative Supervision — Alberto Pérez de Eulate

Graphic Design — Mikel San Juán
