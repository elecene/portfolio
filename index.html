Luciano Castañón Estrada is an Art Director currently working in Madrid.

• Harry Potter — Logo and Design System +

New logo and visual identity for Harry Potter. HBO is bringing the iconic story to a new generation, and we collaborated with them to reimagine one of the most recognizable logos in pop culture and create a fresh brand world for its magical universe.

• Android — Global Rebrand +

Global brand system redesign for Android, introducing updated 3D character assets and a dynamic color palette tailored for multi-device environments.

• Adidas — Alpha Edge Design System +

Digital experience and identity system for Adidas Alpha Edge, blending futuristic sportswear aesthetics with high-performance typography.

• Editorial — Book & Print Design +

Limited edition art publication and editorial layout design exploring grid balance, typography hierarchy, and tactile material finishes.

• Motion — 3D Visual Effects +

3D animation, kinetic typography, and motion design systems for broadcast and digital installations.

• Digital Art — Conceptual Series +

Experimental digital artworks, visual explorations, and graphic design concepts produced for exhibitions and digital releases.
